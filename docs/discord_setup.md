# Discord server blueprint

The build sheet for creating the server. Every piece of public-facing text
below comes from `docs/claims.md`'s WORDING section — **that document is the
source of truth, and no text may make a claim outside its SUPPORTED list.**

Nothing here is automated. `research/publish.py` generates text; **a human
posts it.** There is no bot token, no Discord API call, and no scheduled
publishing anywhere in this repo.

---

> **Crypto-Model — a public research log, not a signal service**
>
> This is an open research project on whether a Bollinger-squeeze + technical

> **What has actually been found so far: nothing that replicates.**

> computed, and the resolved outcome of each one added to a public tally that

> number in this project ever looks impressive, check how many independent

## 2. Channel structure

| channel | who can post | purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **#signals** | **operator only** (locked read-only for members) | OPEN and CLOSE alerts, generated by `publish.py`. Nothing else, ever. |
| **#record** | **operator only** | The monthly transparency post. One post a month, nothing between. |
| **#methodology** | **operator only** | Pinned summary of `claims.md`; the standing explanation of how the project works. |
| **#discussion** | members | Questions and chat. **Not** a place where the operator gives trade opinions — see the operating rules. |

Lock the three operator channels via **Channel Settings → Permissions →
@everyone → Send Messages: OFF**. Members read; they do not post there.

## 3. Pinned posts

### #signals — pin this

> **What this channel is**
>
> Every signal the model publishes, posted as generated, with the exit levels
> it computed. Each one gets a CLOSE post when it resolves — **win or lose,
> with no exceptions and no deletions.**
>
> Two tiers appear here. **BUY** carries the published live record.
> **STRONG_BUY** is the model's rarest tier and is **NOT a validated edge** —
> its post carries the full disclosure, and you should read it every time
> rather than treating the label as a stronger recommendation.
>
> Signals are **long only**. The model also forms short opinions; they are
> logged and never published, because they went 0 for 11.
>
> **No claim is made that any signal will work.** Not financial advice.

### #record — pin this

> **The tally, including the losses**
>
> A transparency post goes up monthly with the complete record: what was
> published, what resolved, net R, the worst losing streak, and the full
> log alongside the published subset.
>
> **Both records are always shown.** The published-long row is what a
> follower experienced. The full-log row is the honest denominator for how
> often the model was wrong. Showing only the flattering one is how selective
> reporting starts.
>
> Nothing is removed from this channel. A bad month stays up.

### #methodology — pin this

> **How this project works**
>
> Every hypothesis is written down in `docs/cleanroom.md` **before it runs**,
> with its exact parameters and what would count as a pass. Results are
> reported afterwards whether they worked or not. Failures get the same
> detail as successes.
>
> **The project currently holds zero supported edge claims.** That is not
> modesty, it is the state of the record: a large number of pre-registered
> hypotheses have been tested and none survived its own pre-registered bar.
>
> The full research log, the claims document, and every line of code are
> public: https://github.com/bembem718ai-stack/Crypto-Model
>
> If a number here ever looks impressive, check how many independent
> episodes it came from. Usually the answer is "not enough".

## 4. Operating rules

**What gets posted, and when**

1. **OPEN alert** — at the first confirmed bar of a published episode. One
   per episode. Generated by `publish.py`, posted to #signals.
2. **CLOSE alert** — when the episode resolves. One per episode, **always**,
   including losses. Posted to #signals.
3. **Monthly transparency post** — once a month to #record, generated by
   `publish.py post`.

**What NEVER gets posted**

- **Shorts.** Logged, never published. The live path is long-only.
- **Refused rows.** `REFUSED_UNTRADABLE` and `SKIPPED_COST_FLOOR` are
  refusals, not signals — they never appear.
- **WATCH / AVOID rows.** Not signals.
- **Anything from the shadow basket.** It is research, it has published
  nothing, and its evaluation is sealed until 30 episodes accrue.
- **Backtest numbers presented as performance.** The research log is public
  and can be linked; it may not be quoted as a track record.
- **Any edited or deleted signal.** A signal that went out stays out, and its
  CLOSE post goes up regardless of outcome.

**The no-DM-advice rule**

> **No trade advice in DMs. Ever. No exceptions, and no exceptions for
> paying members if the server ever has any.**

The reason is not liability theatre. A DM is unlogged and unaudited: it
cannot be added to the public tally, so it is exactly the channel where a
claim could be made that the record would never have to answer for. **If it
is worth saying, it is worth saying in public where it gets counted.**
Answer methodology questions in #discussion; decline position-specific ones.

**Position sizing and personal circumstances**

Do not answer "how much should I put in", "should I use leverage", or
anything requiring knowledge of someone's finances. The correct response is
that this is a research log and those are decisions only they can make.

**Operator disclosure**

> ⚠ **Fill this in before launch, then delete this warning.**
>
> If the operator is **under 18**, say so plainly in the server description
> and in #methodology. It is not a disclaimer to bury — it is context a
> reader is entitled to when deciding how much weight to give the project,
> and disclosing it up front is far better than it emerging later.
>
> Suggested wording: *"This project is run by a student as an
> extracurricular research project. It is not a business and not a
> registered advisor."*
>
> Check the platform rules that apply. Discord's own terms set a minimum age,
> and some jurisdictions restrict who may operate anything that looks like a
> financial service — **even a free one that publishes no advice.** If money
> ever changes hands, that question needs answering properly first.

## 5. Launch checklist

- [ ] Server created; three operator channels locked read-only for @everyone
- [ ] Server description pasted, **with the live-record sentence refreshed**
- [ ] Three pinned posts up
- [ ] `docs/claims.md` linked in #methodology
- [ ] Operator disclosure completed (or deliberately removed as not applicable)
- [ ] `python research/publish.py dry-run` passes locally
- [ ] First transparency post rendered and posted to #record before any signal
      goes out — **the record goes up before the first alert**, so nobody sees
      a signal before they see the tally

The last item is deliberate. A subscriber who sees a signal first, and the
losing record second, has been shown the two in the wrong order.
