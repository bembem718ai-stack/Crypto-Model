Correlation inheritance is essentially where when I trained the placebo it accidentally inherited some of the skill that it was trying to protect against due to the fact that crypto coins typically move in correlation with eachother. It was measured at 2.5pp/yr in equities when I tested it. It couldn't be measured in crypto because the alternative null ( or the time rotation) needs time windows that are longer than 48 months, and my crypto windows are 42 and 35. So in turn some crypto bars were harder than they should've been by some amount.
The tail width caveat is the idea that since the way that the placebo's data is scored and documented, the middle and average consists of many runs, but as you get closer toward the edge and the actual bar percentile, there are naturally less instances or results there, thus there is barely any data, and the edge is essentially just a guess based off of a handful of points. This is where the fidelity axes get confused, as they can verify the middle as being well documented in the placebo, but they cant for the edges - thus creating an unmeasured wobble in the exact spot where every pass or fail decision is made. So thats why #255 being the 99.0th percentile is meaningless when the bar is 99.1667, since the bar isn't precise enough to fit that amount. This can cut both ways, passing things that are meant to fail, and failing those meant to pass.
The breadth problem is the idea that with more coins owned, given that they were independent, I could make more successes with just having more coins - even if the signal is weak. However, upon testing this I realised that the coins are not independent, and the correlation ran between .48 and .71, so they move together. So, when coins are correlated these 26 "seperate" bets become a handful of bets on crypto itself - becoming what would've been just 2 or 3 real bets. This turned out to be more of a limitation, because you can't tell whether or not the strategies failed because they were bad strategies, or the fact that there weren't enough independent bets to detenct a small real edge. What I found with this is that crypto doesn't offer enough actual independent assets for a model to support cross-sectional strategies at the scale I ran the model.
